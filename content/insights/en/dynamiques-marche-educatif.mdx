This note describes lines of inquiry. They are not measured findings, and no external source is cited yet.

BTIS organises a reading of the Burkinabè education sector around a few working questions: demand for education, infrastructure needs, vocational training, higher education and educational innovation.

These questions help situate an opportunity. They are not enough to qualify it. Qualification requires the local context, the stakeholders and the conditions needed to carry a project out.

A quantified study, when it is useful, is produced within a mandate. It is not published here.
