The education sector can cover very different projects. For qualification, BTIS groups them into a few families.

Educational institutions concern creation, development or expansion. Higher education includes universities, specialised schools and some professional training. Vocational training covers skills, employability and continuing education. EdTech brings together educational technologies and digital solutions. Infrastructure covers campuses, training centres and specialised facilities.

The issue is not to choose a label. It is to understand the context, the stakeholders and what must be in place for a project to be carried out. That reading is what BTIS uses to inform a decision.
