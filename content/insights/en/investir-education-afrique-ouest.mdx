This note sets out a way of reading a situation. It advances no figures and it is not a substitute for documented research.

Investing in education in West Africa starts by distinguishing the kind of opportunity: an institution, a training project, higher education, an educational solution or infrastructure.

In Burkina Faso, BTIS supports investors, institutions and partners who want to identify, qualify and structure this kind of project. The work begins with the context, the actors and the conditions required to carry it out.

The decision comes after that: proceed, adapt or set aside. Operational support only makes sense once that qualification is done.
